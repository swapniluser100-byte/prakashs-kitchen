Our story

Cooking the way it's meant to be

Prakash's Kitchen started as a small family stall and grew into a café where regulars know the staff by name. Every recipe here has been in the family for years — the sambar, the pickles, the chai — nothing from a packet.

We keep the menu short on purpose. Fewer dishes, made properly, every single day.
